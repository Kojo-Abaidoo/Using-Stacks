    🍽️ Plate Stack Rush

    Plates of food stack up automatically. Customers ask for a random plate in your stack — serve the top plates to reach it!

    • ⬇ PUSH adds the next plate (auto-stacks up to 4).
    • ⬆ SERVE hands out the top plate only. Match the order to score.
    • ↩ REMOVE TOP pops the top plate without serving (great for digging down).
    • 🗑 TOSS throws the next plate away (customer loses patience).
    • Wrong plate or too slow = lose a ❤️.

    📚 A stack is LIFO: the last plate pushed is the first one you can remove.

    💥 Game Over!
